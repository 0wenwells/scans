How to add a scan

  1. Export your scan as OBJ. If your app also gives you an .mtl file and images, keep them together.
  2. Name it yourname-title, e.g. maya-stairwell.obj. Keep files under 25 MB each.
  3. Open the repository on GitHub, go into the models folder, then Add file → Upload files. For a scan with textures, put all its files in a new folder of their own, e.g. models/maya-stairwell/.
  4. Click Commit changes. Your scan appears here within a couple of minutes.

Use "Check my file" first to see your scan the way the gallery will show it.
